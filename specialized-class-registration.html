Program details

About this program

Specialized classes for individuals or 2-3 students with dance experience are offered for non-competitive and competition students in tap, jazz, ballet, pointe, lyrical, contemporary, and musical theater for ages 5 and up.

Duet/Trio Standard or Competitive: 30-minute weekly dance routine class, $45 monthly per student.

Solo Standard or Competitive: 30-minute weekly class preparing dancers with a competitive or standard solo routine. $475 due in full upon registration.

Private technique-only class: $47 monthly.

Semi-private technique-only class: $45 per student monthly.
