Frequently asked questions

Dance classes near Toledo

Quick answers for families deciding whether Stage Starz is the right fit.

How close is Stage Starz to Toledo?

Stage Starz Academy of Dance is at 6800 Lewis Avenue in Temperance, Michigan, just north of Toledo and convenient for families throughout the Toledo area and Southeast Michigan.

What ages can take dance classes?

Programs begin at age 3 and continue through older and more experienced dancers, with classes organized by age, experience and training level.

Do you offer classes for beginners?

Yes. Beginner-friendly options are available, and our staff can help families choose the most appropriate program when a dancer is brand new.

What styles of dance do you offer?

Programs include ballet, jazz, tap and hip-hop, with additional styles and training opportunities available depending on the dancer's age and level.

Do you offer recreational and competition programs?

Yes. Dancers can enjoy recreational classes or explore competition training for additional performance experience, teamwork and technical development.
