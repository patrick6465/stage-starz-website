2026–27 audition requirements

Stage Starz Competition Team Auditions were scheduled for new hopefuls and returning competition students on Saturday, May 9, 2026.

Dancers must be registered for auditions and pre-registered for the 2026–27 classes they hope to compete.

A $95 2026–27 yearly competition participation fee is due with competition audition online registration.

Please review the studio’s File Downloads for complete audition information concerning attire, expectations, and requirements.

Students are required to pre-register for the 2026–27 classes they hope to compete routines in as part of the audition process.

If you are viewing this after the scheduled May 9 audition date and need placement guidance, contact Stage Starz before registering so the studio can advise you on current options.
