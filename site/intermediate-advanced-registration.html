About this program

JUNIOR COMPETITION TEAM (ages 9-13 Intermediate Level). Please note - AUDITION REQUIRED.

Stage Starz Junior Competition Jazz/Lyrical group combo: $80 monthly for 90-minute weekly class block including 30 minutes required Ballet Tech, 30 minutes Lyrical Routine, and 30 minutes Jazz Routine.

Stage Starz Junior Competition TAP Group: $55 monthly for 60-minute weekly class.

Stage Starz Junior Competition HIP HOP Group: $38 monthly for 30-minute weekly class.

Stage Starz Mixed-Age Musical Theater Group Competition Routine: $38 monthly for 45-minute weekly competition novelty musical theater character-jazz style routine.

Intermediate Comp Ballet Tech: 45-minute supplemental Ballet Tech class focused on advancing and refining technical training. $38 monthly.

Intermediate Flexibility & Conditioning Tech Class: 45-minute weekly class focused on stretching, flexibility, and extensions. $38 monthly.

Intermediate Turns, Leaps, & Jumps Tech Class: 45-minute weekly tech class focused on turns, leaps, and jumps. $38 monthly.

Stage Starz Junior Competition Duo/Trio: $45 monthly for 30-minute weekly class preparing dancers with a competitive duo or trio routine.
