Teen Competition Team classes & tuition

Teen Jazz/Contemporary Combo: $85 monthly for a 90-minute weekly class block including 45 minutes of Teen Competition Lyrical routine and 45 minutes of Teen Competition Jazz routine.

Int2–Advanced Comp Ballet Tech: $38 monthly. One weekly 45-minute Ballet Tech class is required for Teen Competition Team groups and for applicable solos, duos, and trios.

Teen Tap Competition Group: $55 monthly for a 60-minute weekly class block including 30 minutes of required Teen Competition Tap Tech and 30 minutes of Teen Competition Tap routine. The required Tap Tech is built into this class block.

Int2–Advanced Tap Tech: $38 monthly for Teen competitors who are not enrolled in the Competition Tap Group and require weekly Tap Tech.

Mixed-Age Musical Theater Competition Group: $42 monthly for a 45-minute weekly musical-theater character-jazz competition routine. This group is available to Teen Competition Jazz/Lyrical combo members or dancers meeting the required competition Ballet Tech requirement.

Int2–Advanced Flexibility & Conditioning: $38 monthly for a 45-minute weekly class focused on stretching, flexibility, extensions, clean lines, and control.

Int2–Advanced Turns, Leaps & Jumps: $38 monthly for a 45-minute weekly technique class focused on stationary and across-the-floor turns, leaps, and jumps.

Competition Duo/Trio: $45 monthly per dancer for a 30-minute weekly class preparing a competitive duo or trio routine.

Solo: $475 paid in full upon registration. 2026–27 solo spots are currently sold out.

Important tech requirement: Solo and duo/trio competitors who are not already receiving the required competition tech through a group must take the applicable weekly Ballet Tech or Tap Tech class.
