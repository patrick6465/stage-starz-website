Teen / Senior team classes & tuition

Senior competition groups, solos, duos and trios for Jazz, Contemporary, Lyrical and Musical Theater require one 45-minute weekly Intermediate 2–Advanced Ballet Tech class.

Tap Group: $55 monthly for a 60-minute weekly tap competition group routine class with Tap Tech included.

Jazz Group: $40 monthly for a 45-minute weekly jazz competition group routine class.

Contemporary/Lyrical Group: $40 monthly for a 45-minute weekly class.

Mixed-Age Musical Theater Group Competition Routine: $38 monthly for a 45-minute weekly class.

Intermediate 2–Advanced Flexibility & Conditioning: $38 monthly.

Intermediate 2–Advanced Turns, Leaps & Jumps: $38 monthly.

Competition Duo/Trio: $45 monthly per student for a 30-minute weekly class.

Intermediate 2–Advanced Ballet Tech: required weekly Ballet Tech for Teen and Senior competitors.
